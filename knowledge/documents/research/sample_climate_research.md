# Climate Forecasting Methods

## Statistical Methods

Traditional climate forecasting relies on statistical models that analyze historical patterns. These methods include regression analysis, time series decomposition, and principal component analysis.

## Machine Learning Approaches

Deep learning models have shown significant promise in climate forecasting. Key architectures include:

### Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)
LSTM networks are well-suited for time series forecasting due to their ability to capture long-term dependencies in sequential data.

### Transformer Models
Transformer architectures with self-attention mechanisms have been applied to climate forecasting, showing improved performance for multi-variable prediction tasks.

## Digital Twin Technology

Digital twins create virtual representations of physical climate systems. They integrate real-time observations with simulation models to provide a comprehensive view of the climate system.

## Explainable AI in Climate Science

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values provide interpretability for machine learning models by quantifying the contribution of each input feature to the model's predictions.
