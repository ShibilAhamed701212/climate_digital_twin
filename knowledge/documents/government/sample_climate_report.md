# Karnataka Climate Report 2025

## Overview

Karnataka experiences a tropical climate with distinct wet and dry seasons. The state receives majority of its rainfall from the Southwest Monsoon (June-September).

## Rainfall Patterns

The average annual rainfall in Karnataka is approximately 1,200 mm. Coastal districts like Udupi and Dakshina Kannada receive over 3,000 mm annually, while the interior districts of Bijapur and Raichur receive less than 600 mm.

## Temperature Trends

Summer temperatures in Karnataka typically range from 26°C to 38°C. The highest temperatures are recorded in April and May, particularly in the eastern plains.

## Key Climate Risks

1. Drought: Interior districts face frequent drought conditions due to rainfall variability.
2. Flooding: Coastal and riverine districts experience flooding during heavy monsoon years.
3. Heatwaves: Eastern plains experience heatwaves during pre-monsoon months (March-May).
