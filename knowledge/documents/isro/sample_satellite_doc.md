# INSAT-3DR Satellite Products

## Overview

INSAT-3DR is a dedicated meteorological satellite operated by ISRO. It provides continuous observations of the Indian region for weather forecasting and climate monitoring.

## Key Products

### Rainfall Estimation
The INSAT-3DR provides quantitative precipitation estimates using infrared and water vapor channels. The estimates are calibrated against IMD rain gauge data.

### Sea Surface Temperature
SST products are derived from thermal infrared channels at 4 km spatial resolution. These are used for monsoon prediction and cyclone tracking.

### Cloud Top Temperature
Cloud top temperatures are used to estimate convective intensity and rainfall potential.

## Applications

1. Real-time weather monitoring
2. Cyclone warning and tracking
3. Monsoon monitoring and prediction
4. Agricultural advisory services
5. Flood warning systems
