# NDMA flood response — operational summary

This note summarizes public NDMA flood guidance for Copilot grounding. It is not a substitute for official orders.

## Incident command

State and district disaster management authorities coordinate search, rescue, and relief. NDMA issues guidelines; SDMA/DDMA execute. The Climate Digital Twin disaster overlay is an **information product**, not a dispatch system of record.

## Observed inundation vs climate flood risk

Rainfall-based flood **risk scores** estimate hazard from meteorological drivers. Satellite **flood extent** maps water that is already present. Operators must not treat the two numbers as the same quantity.

## Relief priorities (high level)

1. Life safety and medical facilities
2. Access (roads, bridges)
3. Shelter and drinking water
4. Restoration of essential services

Population exposure estimates require census or WorldPop joins. If those joins are missing, Copilot must say population is unavailable.
